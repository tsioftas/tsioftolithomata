    Tree of Life

    Taallalt, Issoumour Mountain

    Age
    ~420 million years ago
    Period
    Late Silurian (Pridoli)
    Formation
    Unknown
    Depositional environment
    Open marine shelf to deeper offshore setting below storm wave base
    Fossil types
    Crinoids, orthoconic cephalopods, other marine invertebrates
    Paleoecology highlights
    Calm offshore waters, fine sediment accumulation, floating crinoid colonies with gas-filled loboliths, episodic transport and burial, low-energy seafloor conditions

    Picture an offshore sea stretching far into the horizon. The water is clear and sunlit near the surface, fading into deeper blue below. Fine carbonate mud and silt settle slowly onto the seafloor, forming pale, layered sediments that blanket the landscape.

    The environment is stable and low-energy, with only occasional disturbances. Over time, this calm marine setting builds thick successions of limestone and shale, preserving dead organisms that sink to the bottom, capturing a snapshot of a distinctive Silurian ecosystem in the final stages before the Devonian world began.
