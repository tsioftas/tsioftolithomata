    Alnif (Ktaoua formation)

    • Age: ~450 million years ago
    • Period: Late Ordovician (Katian)
    • Formation: Ktaoua Formation
    • Depositional environment: Shallow marine shelf (storm-dominated ramp)
    • Fossil types: Trilobites, brachiopods, bryozoans, orthoconic cephalopods, crinoid fragments
    • Paleoecology highlights: Shelly seafloor communities, occasional patchy carbonate shoals, storm-buried fauna, limited algal mats

    Imagine standing beneath the surface of a shallow, sunlit sea. The water is clear but carries a soft golden haze from stirred-up sand and silt. Beneath you stretches a vast seabed of rippled sand, shaped by waves and distant storms. Low mounds of finer mud and scattered carbonate patches mark spots where shelly debris once gathered. There’s no land in sight; just the gently sloping seafloor disappearing into deeper blue. This was a calm moment between storms, in a world where seas rolled over an ancient shelf at the edge of Gondwana, long before plants took hold on land.
