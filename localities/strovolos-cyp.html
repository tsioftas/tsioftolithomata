    🌳Το δεντρόν του βκιού

    [αμετάφραστο]

    • Ηλικία: 3.6-2.58 εκατομμύρια χρόνια
    • Περίοδος: Έψιμον Πλειότζ̌αινον
    • Σχηματισμός: Lefkosia Marl Member (Lefkosia Formation)
    • Περιβάλλον εναπόθεσης: Open marine shelf of the Mesaoria Basin with marl-dominated sedimentation, periodic water-column stratification and freshwater input episodes. The record is now subsurface, covered by Quaternary alluvium.
    • Τύποι απολιθωμάτων: Bivalves, gastropods, scaphopods, barnacles, echinoids, corals, conifer remains, calcareous nannofossils and planktonic foraminifera
    • Παλαιοοικολογία: Warm Pliocene sea with surface temperatures around 26°C, open marine shelf with stratified water column, periodic freshwater input producing sapropelic-marly alternations, episodic seafloor ecological stress, progressive shift to shallower waters toward the end of the Pliocene
