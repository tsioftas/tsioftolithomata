      Striatolamia macrota is an extinct shark known for its large (19-38mm) striated teeth. Found in lower-middle Eocene shallow marine deposits, its teeth differ from other Striatolamia species by size and root structure. The species is estimated to have reached about 350cm in length.

      Etymology

      The species name “striatolamia macrota” adds the Greek adjective μακρός (“long”, feminine μακρά), referring to the species’ characteristically large, elongated teeth (up to 38 mm) which distinguish it from related species with smaller dentition. Originally described by Agassiz (as Otodus macrotus) in 1843.
